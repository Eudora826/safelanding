Why Our
Website?

Housing scams are tricky, and hard to differentiate.

SafeLanding fuses a live rule/LLM scam detector with a community threat-intelligence database. Your message is checked against real URL heuristics, scam-wording rules, and reports other renters have already filed - so you find out both whether a message looks like a scam, and whether this exact landlord, phone number, or address has been reported before.

    About SafeLanding

    SafeLanding is a rental-scam detector for non-native speakers in the Netherlands. The live analyzer answers "does this message look like a scam?". The community database answers "have we seen this specific scammer or pattern before?". Fusing the two gives one verdict that is both pattern-aware and memory-aware.

    How to Use?

    1. 1

      Paste the suspicious message, a listing link, or upload a screenshot of the chat / email / document.

    2. 2

      Press the orange arrow. We estimate the risk and show you exactly which warning signs were found, in your own language.

    3. 3

      Open "More Info" on any risk card to see matching scam patterns, similar documented cases, and recommended next steps - then report it to help the next person.
